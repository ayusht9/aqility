Your air. Your next move. 🌿

I built Aqility for PromptWars: Virtual by Hack2skill — an AI-powered air-quality companion that connects environmental readings with practical daily choices.

The latest update adds a cleaner-air action plan:
• Routines for commuting, staying home, family trips, and community activities
• Lower-emission travel alternatives and habits
• Indoor-air tips with ventilation and temperature precautions
• “Why this fits” and “How it helps” explanations, with EPA source links
• A 24-hour outdoor planner
• GPT-5 nano summaries and smallest.ai spoken briefings

The plan distinguishes reducing pollution at its source from reducing what we breathe. It explains the likely impact without inventing carbon savings or indoor AQI measurements.

Privacy was a priority: personal health-profile fields stay in the browser and are excluded from AI and voice requests.
